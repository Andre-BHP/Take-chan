家系ラーメンとは

What is Iekei

The broth
Pork bones boiled until creamy, with soy sauce and a layer of fat on top.
The noodles
Thick and straight, so the broth clings to every bite.
The name
家系 (iekei) comes from the Yokohama ramen shops whose names end in 家, “house”.

According to its listing, Take-chan’s noodles and broth are made in Japan and imported.

作り方

How an Iekei bowl is made

Four steps of the style, as in Yokohama.

四 · The bowl · swipe the steps

Illustration of the style

  1. 一

    Step 1: The broth

    Pork bones boiled hard until the broth turns thick, creamy and almost white.

  2. 二

    Step 2: The tare

    A soy sauce base goes at the bottom of the bowl: it gives the color and the salty taste.

  3. 三

    Step 3: The noodles

    Thick and straight, so the broth clings to it. Ordered firm, regular or soft.

  4. 四

    Step 4: The bowl

    Spinach, three sheets of nori standing on the rim, chashu and half a marinated egg.

質問

Frequently asked questions

What is Iekei ramen?

A ramen style born in Yokohama: pork bone broth with soy sauce, rich and topped with a layer of fat, with thick, straight noodles. It’s served with spinach, chashu and three sheets of nori.

How do I order?

On the menu, press the buttons on the ticket machine and choose your noodles, flavor and fat. In this proposal, sending via WhatsApp is a demo.

What does ordering it “your way” mean?

In Iekei style you usually choose how firm the noodles are (硬め firm, 普通 regular or 柔らかめ soft), how strong the flavor is and how much fat it has. First time? Order everything regular.

Where are you?

At Calz. Córdoba 24, Super Manzana El Álamo, at the corner of Niños Héroes, in San Pedro Tlaquepaque. The Americana location (Prisciliano Sánchez 1110) is still to be confirmed.

What are your hours?

In Tlaquepaque: every day, from 12:00 to 17:00.

Do you do takeout?

This proposal includes pickup orders via WhatsApp; the restaurant still has to confirm it.

What does an Iekei bowl usually contain?

By style, it usually contains pork (in the broth and the chashu), wheat (in the noodles), soy (in the tare) and egg if ordered with a marinated egg. If you have an allergy, ask at the restaurant before ordering.
