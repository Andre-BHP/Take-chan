Privacy notice

This prototype neither stores nor sends any data. The name you type in your order is only used to put the message together on your device.

What is kept in your browser

  • Whether you saw the intro, so it doesn’t repeat during the same visit.
  • Whether you prefer the menu as a machine or a list, and the notices you closed.
  • A copy of the menu so it opens offline.

None of this leaves your device, and you can delete it from your browser settings. There are no cookies and no analytics.

Third-party services

TikTok videos only load if you tap the button; from then on, TikTok’s policy applies. The Google Maps buttons open Google Maps.
